## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/blustery-bulwark-of-splorr/ (once Pages is enabled; also add it as an external link on the page)

**Blustery Bulwark of SPLORR!!** is a metaphor about hot air, in the shape of a tile-based roguelike.

One night a gale flattens yer farm. Next morning the herald reads a proclamation: His Lordship, as foretold, has blown down the farm of the insolent. So you pack what you can carry and set off up the bulwark, floor by floor, where the wind gets worse the higher you go.

- The wind is real. Gusts push loose things, light creatures and you (unless yer load is heavy enough to anchor you), blow out yer torch and bend yer spells in flight.
- No classes. Four attributes, a spell of yer choice at every level.
- Everything you carry has weight and bulk, and goes in a pack, a sack, a belt or a purse.
- Unidentified potions and scrolls, curses that will not come off, a sage, a temple and a junk shop that buys anything for a pittance.
- Die, and you wake in town. Yer pack stays where you fell. Die again before you fetch it, and it is gone.
